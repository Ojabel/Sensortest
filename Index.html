How to Use This Sensor Test

1. Click "Start Sensor Test" to begin testing all available sensors

2. Grant permission when your browser asks for sensor access

3. Move and rotate your phone to see the sensors in action

4. Test individual sensors by:

↻ Rotate device for Orientation & Gyroscope
📳 Shake device for Accelerometer
🧭 Move in different directions for Magnetometer
🔍 Cover the top of phone for Proximity
💡 Change lighting for Ambient Light

Note: Not all sensors are available on all devices. Sensor availability depends on your phone hardware and browser support.
